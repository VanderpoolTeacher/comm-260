5 / 12

A departure is not an apology

"I planned five interior shots and got three. The room was smaller than I remembered and two angles did not exist. I used the time on the exterior instead — the treatment had one line about it, and the piece now opens with it."

That is a decision.

"Some shots didn't work out."

That is an apology.

Alone is not easier

The break

This phase loses two sessions.

Contact time, not content. Weeks 7–9 already happened.

What goes away is somebody to ask while you are stuck.

Two rules for the break

Book equipment for the whole phase — including the days nobody is on campus.

Do not plan to shoot during the break unless your subject has already confirmed.
