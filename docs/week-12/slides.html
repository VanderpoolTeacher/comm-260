7 / 17

Two ways it fails

"I make content for people."

Too broad. It rules nothing out.

"I'm a videographer."

A job title. It says what you are called, not who the work is for.

8 / 17

And one that works

"I make short documentary portraits for regional arts organisations."

It tells you what to shoot, who for, and what conventions apply.

11 / 17

Adjectives are not values

Not checkable Checkable
"Warm and approachable" #C4703A primary, #F4EFE8 ground
"Clean, modern type" Söhne 600 headings, 400 at 17px body
"Intimate portraits" Shallow depth — f/2.8 or wider — every portrait
"Good pacing" No shot over 6 seconds. Music never under speech
