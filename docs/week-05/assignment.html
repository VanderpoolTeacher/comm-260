Week 5 Assignment — Three recordings and a comparison

COMM 260 · Introduction to Digital Media Production

Formative · submitted and critiqued · 0% of your course grade

Required, and week 6 depends on it. Must be submitted before week 6's module summative. Week 6 builds a piece from these recordings — if you do not record this week, you have nothing to edit.

If you miss this week, you must record your own three setups before week 6's lab, on your own time, with your own booked equipment.

What to submit

  1. Three raw recordings — lavalier, shotgun, handheld — plus your room tone
  2. A written comparison, which is your tools statement for this week

The comparison

For each of the three setups:

  • Which microphone, and why that one for that situation
  • What gain you set, and how you knew it was right
  • What you heard on headphones before you recorded, and what you changed

Then: which of the three would you use for this subject, and what does that cost you?

How this is graded

Assessed on your tools statement — mic choice, gain, and why, for each of the three setups. Corroborated by lab observation of your setup. This is LO 2 evidence.

The reason is what counts, not the list.

"Lav, −9 dBFS, because the subject was seated and I needed the mic close without it in frame" — evidences competency

"Lav, −9 dBFS" — evidences record-keeping

What does not count

  • A list of settings. That is record-keeping.
  • "It sounded better." Say what you heard.
  • No room tone. It is part of the submission.
  • A test you listened to and changed nothing about. That is not monitoring.
  • Recordings you have not listened back to.

Use the glossary

This week: Raw sources, Tools statement, Lab observation, Capture settings.

Rubric

LO 2 — Industry-standard tools

Demonstrate competency with industry-standard tools for digital media production, including cameras, audio equipment, and editing software.

Level
Exceeds Settings justified against the situation, and you say what the chosen setting gave up
Meets Operates the equipment unaided. Tools statement gives settings with reasons for this subject
Approaching Settings recorded accurately with no reasoning attached, or operation needed prompting
Not yet Defaults accepted; cannot say what was set or why

LO 5 — Audio

Record and edit audio with attention to clarity, quality, and storytelling impact.

Level
Exceeds A structural decision changes how the piece lands, and you can say what it cost
Meets Audibly better than the raw — clear, and shaped by a structural decision. Raw sources submitted
Approaching Cleanly cut, but says what the raw said. Nothing structural happened
Not yet Processed speech, noise reduction as the main edit, or no raw sources

The same four levels apply to every assignment in this course. Full descriptors and the whole map of which assignment evidences what: competency rubric.
