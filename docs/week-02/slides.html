Outcomes

By the end of this week you can

  1. Name the compositional decision in an image someone else made
  2. Find an image that demonstrates a given term — and defend why it qualifies

  4. Recognise frozen and blurred motion, and say what each conveys
  5. Name what gives an image its subject emphasis — and say which means is doing the most work
