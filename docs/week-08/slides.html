Outcomes

By the end of this week you can

  1. Write a treatment and build a shot list from it
  2. Storyboard a sequence
  3. Schedule a shoot and run it — book the equipment, sequence the day, keep it on track
  4. Hold a defined role on a production someone else is directing
  5. Coordinate with your team and record what you were responsible for
