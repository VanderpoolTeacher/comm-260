Building a Media Producer's Presence — Project Rubric

COMM 260 · Step 3b of the Course Build Process

Covers components A–H. The presentation (I, 15%) is scored on the Presentation Rubric.

Every Meets descriptor is the criterion from Assessment Evidence, stated as an observable standard.

A. Brand Foundation — 10%

Container — evidences no outcome. Scored because it governs everything else; weighted low because it proves nothing about the course.

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Every rule is specific, and each traces to the stated positioning. Covers cases the student had not yet shot.
Meets Style guide gives specific values — hex codes, typefaces, aperture or lighting rules, tempo or pacing limits. A photograph, edit, or mix can be checked against it. Positioning, audience, voice, and output all stated.
Approaching Adjectives instead of values: "warm," "clean," "modern," "good pacing." Nothing downstream can be checked against it.
Not yet Logo only, or generic design advice that would fit any producer.

B. Photography Set — 10%

Evidences: LO 3 · 12 images, JPEG, longest edge 2400 px, sRGB

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Both halves met, and the set functions as a set — the images work together as a body of work, not as twelve individually competent frames.
Meets All 12 images delivered to spec. They meet stated technical specifications and demonstrably serve the stated creative intent. Tools statement explains what was used and why those settings for this subject.
Approaching One half only — technically clean with no discernible intent, or expressive but out of focus or unusably exposed. Or fewer than 12.
Not yet Neither half, or intent written after the shoot to fit what was captured.

C. Audio Piece — 10%

Evidences: LO 5 · 2–3 minutes, WAV 48 kHz/24-bit + MP3 320 kbps

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Edit is clean and the structural decisions — what was cut, what was held — visibly shape how the piece lands.
Meets Runs 2–3 minutes. The edit measurably improves on the source, and edit decisions serve the story rather than only tidiness. Raw sources submitted. Tools statement explains mic choice, gain, and edit decisions with reasons.
Approaching Edit is tidy but structurally inert — noise removed, nothing shaped. Or the improvement over source is not demonstrable. Or outside the length.
Not yet Raw sources withheld — the outcome cannot be assessed without them — or no meaningful edit performed.

D. Video Piece — 15%

Evidences: LO 4 · 60–90 seconds, H.264 MP4, 1080p, 24 fps

Level Descriptor
Exceeds All three phases evidenced, and departures from plan are not only identified but justified against what was learned on the shoot.
Meets Runs 60–90 seconds to spec. The finished piece is traceable to the plan, and departures are identified as deliberate decisions rather than accidents. Planning documents and project file submitted. Tools statement explains camera and edit settings with reasons.
Approaching Plan exists but bears no relationship to the finished piece, or departures are unacknowledged — plan and result simply differ.
Not yet No planning documentation. A strong edit of unplanned footage does not satisfy "plan, shoot, and edit."

E. Landing Page — 20%

Evidences: LO 7, LO 6 · published to a live URL

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Interdependence is structural — the media carry different parts of the meaning — and the mode justification identifies a cost the student accepted knowingly.
Meets Page is live at a working URL and holds all required media. Removing any one medium breaks the piece. The mode justification names what the chosen structure makes possible and what it costs.
Approaching All three media present but one is removable without loss — assembly rather than integration. Or the justification describes the choice without weighing the alternative.
Not yet A medium is absent, the URL does not resolve, or the justification restates what was made.

Not assessed: code quality, browser compatibility, visual polish beyond the student's own style guide.

F. Positioning Statement — 5%

Evidences: LO 10 · 300–500 words

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Points at specific choices in the work as evidence of the conventions claimed — "this framing, because that is how the field shoots it."
Meets Names the target professional context specifically and the conventions it observes, and the work visibly matches the claim. Originality is demonstrable.
Approaching Context named but fit asserted rather than shown — the statement claims conventions the work does not display.
Not yet No named context, or work is a tutorial output, template fill, or restyled existing work.

G. Crew Log — 10%

Evidences: LO 9 · Process — corroborated · 3 sessions, 3 different producers

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Log shows the student adapting to different producers' working methods, and sign-offs describe specific contributions rather than confirming attendance.
Meets Three sessions with three different producers across weeks 12–14, each recording role, responsibility, and one problem handled. Signed by the producer crewed for.
Approaching Log is present but thin or uncorroborated — attendance recorded without responsibility, sign-offs missing, or fewer than three different producers.
Not yet No crewing, or a log of the student's own productions. Own work is not evidence for this outcome.

H. Critique Participation — 5%

Evidences: LO 8 · Observed · 2 classmates × 4 checkpoints

Level Descriptor
Exceeds Critique consistently identifies the underlying cause of a weakness, not only its symptom, and does so across all three dimensions.
Meets Two classmates critiqued at each checkpoint. Specific enough to act on, addresses technical quality, aesthetic design, and narrative effectiveness, and distinguishes preference from judgment.
Approaching Specific but narrow — defaults to the most visible dimension — or blurs preference and judgment, or misses checkpoints.
Not yet Absent, or limited to affirmation. Self-assessment does not substitute.

The tools statement

Components B, C, and D each require a tools statement: equipment and software used, settings chosen, and why those settings for that situation.

It is the evidence for LO 2 and is scored inside its component, not separately. A component whose tools statement only lists settings without reasons cannot reach Meets, however good the media is — listing settings is record-keeping; explaining them is competency.

Counts "f/2.8 to drop the cluttered background behind her"
Does not count "f/2.8, 1/250, ISO 400"
