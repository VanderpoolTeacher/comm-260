Outcomes

By the end of this week you can

  1. Integrate three media into a landing page that passes the removal test
  2. Justify your linear or non-linear structure with both its gains and its costs
  3. Write a 300–500 word positioning statement naming your context and the conventions you follow
  4. Defend your decisions live, including ones you did not prepare

What did it cost?
