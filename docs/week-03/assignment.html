Week 3 Assignment — Six images to an intent

COMM 260 · Introduction to Digital Media Production

Formative · submitted and critiqued · 0% of your course grade

This is required, and week 4 depends on it. It must be submitted before week 4's module summative. Week 4's lab edits these images — if you do not shoot this week, you have nothing to edit, no before/after to submit, and no work of your own in the first graded critique.

If you miss this week, you must shoot your own set before week 4's lab, on your own time.

What to submit

  1. 6 images, selected down from a shoot of at least twenty-five
  2. Your written creative intent — the one you handed in before shooting, unchanged
  3. Your tools statement — settings, with reasons

Keep your full shoot and your originals. They are not submitted, and week 4 needs them.

How this is graded

Assessed on whether a partner can read your intent from the images alone, without being told what it was.

Plus your tools statement — settings with reasons.

On the tools statement, the reason is what counts — not the list.

"f/2.8 to drop the cluttered background behind her" — evidences competency

"f/2.8, 1/250, ISO 400" — evidences record-keeping

What does not count

  • An intent written after the shoot. Yours was collected before you shot. That is why.
  • An intent that cannot fail. "To show emotion" is satisfied by any six images.
  • Six versions of one image. Selecting down is not picking the best frame six times.
  • Emphasis by placement alone. At least two of the five means, in the same frame.
  • Settings with no reasons. A list is record-keeping.
  • A partner who guessed from context. If they knew your subject already, that is not the images reading.

Use the glossary

Check your words against the glossary before you submit.

This week: Creative intent, Subject emphasis, Subject placement, Capture settings, Tools statement.

Rubric

LO 3 — Photography

Capture high-definition photography that reflects technical accuracy and creative vision.

Level
Exceeds Both halves met, and the images function as one body of work
Meets Meets stated technical specifications and demonstrably serves the stated creative intent
Approaching One half only — technically clean with no discernible intent, or expressive but unusably exposed or focused
Not yet Neither half — or the intent was written after the shoot to fit what was captured

LO 2 — Industry-standard tools

Demonstrate competency with industry-standard tools for digital media production, including cameras, audio equipment, and editing software.

Level
Exceeds Settings justified against the situation, and you say what the chosen setting gave up
Meets Operates the equipment unaided. Tools statement gives settings with reasons for this subject
Approaching Settings recorded accurately with no reasoning attached, or operation needed prompting
Not yet Defaults accepted; cannot say what was set or why

The same four levels apply to every assignment in this course. Full descriptors and the whole map of which assignment evidences what: competency rubric.
